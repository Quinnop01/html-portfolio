My Absolute Favorite Movies According to Ashley

My top 3 movies of all time

Speed (1994)

This one is an oldie but it is still one of my favorite action movies with Keanu Reeves and Sandra Bullock.

KPop Demon Hunters (2025)

The music is so amazing and I love all the characters, even the demons.
I still hum the songs even after watching the movie!

Unstoppable (2010)

Another action movie with Denzel Washington and Chris Pine about a runaway train.
The events of the movie are based on a true story about a runaway train incident named the "Crazy Eights".
